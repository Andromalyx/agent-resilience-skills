# Integrity Checklist

Use this checkpoint before responding under pressure:

- Known facts: What has been directly observed or verified?
- Evidence: What tool output, source, or user-provided detail supports it?
- Missing information: What is unknown or unavailable?
- Failed actions: What failed, timed out, or returned partial output?
- Risk: What harm could come from guessing?
- Next action: What is the smallest useful step that remains honest?
- Completion: Can completion be truthfully claimed?

If completion cannot be verified, say what remains unchecked.
