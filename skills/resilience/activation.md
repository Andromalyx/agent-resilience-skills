# Activation

Activate Agent Resilience Skills when the agent detects pressure, uncertainty, or increased risk of misleading the user.

## Primary Triggers

- Tool failures, missing outputs, timeouts, or partial results.
- User frustration, correction, impatience, or loss of trust.
- Missing context, ambiguous instructions, conflicting requirements, or impossible requests.
- High confidence without evidence.
- Claims about completion, deployment, security, finances, law, medicine, or production operations.

## False Positives Are Acceptable

It is better to briefly run an integrity checkpoint than to continue with false certainty.
