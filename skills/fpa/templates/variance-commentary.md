# Variance Commentary Template

```text
{Line}: {actual} vs {baseline_name} {baseline} -> {variance} ({variance_pct}), {F|U}.
Evidence: {top contributors from GL drill-down: counterparty/memo/JE + amount}.
Driver: {owner-confirmed cause | [DRIVER: confirm with budget owner]}.
Nature: {one-off | timing | run-rate | unknown}.
Forecast impact: {amount + period | n/a | pending driver confirmation}.
Owner / next step: {name or role} - {action} by {date}.
```

Rules:
- One bullet per material **leaf** line; one headline sentence for net income / EBITDA.
- Evidence and driver are separate fields. Evidence comes from data; driver comes from
  a person or document.
- If data quality is not `high`, start the section with the caveat.
