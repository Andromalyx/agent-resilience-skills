# Data Request Template (when inputs are missing)

Use when the analysis is blocked or would be low-confidence. Ask for the minimum.

```text
To give you a reliable {P&L / variance / forecast}, I need:

Required
1. G/L detail or trial balance for {periods} - columns: date or period, account,
   account name, debit & credit (or signed amount), JE id, memo, vendor/customer.
2. Which periods are actuals vs budget, and the currency.

Strongly recommended
3. Chart-of-accounts mapping (account -> P&L line) if your CoA is non-standard.
4. Budget / forecast by account and month (state if signs are natural or debit/credit).
5. Your materiality threshold (default: 10,000 and 10%).

What I can do now with what you sent: {partial scope, clearly labeled}.
```
